Education & Learning

Heritage Resources

A curated directory of universities, courses, and programmes across India offering education in heritage conservation, architectural conservation, and heritage management — to help students and professionals navigate the field.

🏛️

Architecture & Conservation

Master's Degree Programmes in India

09

New Delhi

School of Planning and Architecture (SPA), Delhi

M.Arch. in Architectural Conservation

One of India's premier institutes for architecture and planning, SPA Delhi offers a dedicated postgraduate programme in Architectural Conservation — training professionals in the theory, practice, and technology of conserving historic buildings and environments.

Govt. Institute Visit Website

Bhopal

School of Planning and Architecture (SPA), Bhopal

M.Arch. in Conservation

Established as an Institute of National Importance, SPA Bhopal trains students in conservation theory, philosophy, process, and techniques for maintenance and management of heritage structures.

Govt. Institute Visit Website

Vijayawada

School of Planning and Architecture (SPA), Vijayawada

M.Arch. in Sustainable Architecture

With a focus on sustainable architecture that incorporates heritage-related aspects, this programme bridges contemporary design thinking with the conservation of South India's rich building traditions.

Govt. Institute Visit Website

Roorkee

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee

M.Arch. in Conservation and Regeneration

IIT Roorkee's architecture department offers a specialized M.Arch. programme combining conservation theory and practice with regeneration strategies for historic buildings and urban environments.

IIT / Govt. Visit Website

Ahmedabad

CEPT University

M.Arch. / M.Des. in Conservation and Regeneration

One of India's leading architecture schools, CEPT's programme in Conservation and Regeneration is widely regarded as one of the most rigorous and comprehensive in the country, with strong links to practice.

Autonomous Visit Website

New Delhi

National Museum Institute (NMI), Delhi

MA & PhD in Conservation, Preservation, and Heritage Management

NMI offers full-time MA and PhD programmes blending theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical training, preparing graduates for careers in conservation of art and cultural heritage objects.

Govt. Institute Visit Website

Hyderabad

Jawaharlal Nehru Architecture and Fine Arts University (JNAFAU)

M.Arch. in Architectural Conservation

JNAFAU's conservation programme focuses on the rich architectural heritage of the Deccan and South India, equipping students with the tools to document, analyse, and conserve historic structures.

State University Visit Website

Ahmedabad

Anant National University

M.Arch. in Conservation

Anant National University brings an interdisciplinary, design-led approach to conservation education, integrating sustainability and community perspectives with architectural heritage practice.

Private University Visit Website

Jaipur

Aayojan School of Architecture, Jaipur

M.Arch. in Architectural Conservation

A premier architecture school in Rajasthan, Aayojan offers a two-year full-time Masters in Architectural Conservation, with particular strength in the conservation of Rajasthan's extraordinary built heritage.

Private Institute Visit Website

Management & Policy

Heritage Management Programmes

06

New Delhi

Delhi Institute of Heritage Research and Management (DIHRM)

MA in Heritage Management

DIHRM is Delhi's dedicated heritage management institute, offering an MA programme that equips students with skills in documentation, policy, management, and advocacy for Delhi's extraordinarily rich built and intangible heritage. The institute works closely with government bodies and international organisations.

Delhi Govt. Visit Website

Ahmedabad

Ahmedabad University

MMS in Heritage Management

This management-focused programme builds a critical understanding of heritage and a managerial approach to the heritage sector — covering tangible, intangible, cultural, and natural heritage. Graduates have been placed with the Aga Khan Trust for Culture, INTACH, UNESCO, and IITs.

Private University Visit Website

Dehradun

Wildlife Institute of India (WII)

M.Sc. in Heritage Conservation and Management

A unique two-year residential programme integrating ecological sciences and heritage studies through theoretical modules, workshops, electives, and fieldwork. Recognised by UNESCO and listed on the World Heritage Centre's university network.

MoEFCC / Govt. Visit Website

Bhubaneswar

Utkal University of Culture

Master's in Cultural Heritage and Conservation

A two-year programme focused on the study, preservation, and promotion of cultural heritage, equipping students with knowledge and hands-on experience to address the challenges of conserving Odisha's rich cultural legacy.

State University Visit Website

New Delhi

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi (AUD)

MA in Conservation, Preservation & Heritage Management

Offered through AUD's School of Heritage Research and Management, this interdisciplinary MA programme draws on history, archaeology, and social sciences to develop well-rounded heritage professionals. Also offers MA in Archaeology & Heritage Management.

State University Visit Website

Bengaluru

Srishti Manipal Institute of Art, Design and Technology

MA in Urban Design, Sustainability & Conservation (UNESCO Chair)

This programme, established under the UNESCO Chair in Culture, Habitat, and Sustainable Development, delivers familiarity with international heritage frameworks and connects conservation with urban design and sustainability.

UNESCO Chair Visit Website

Digital Learning

Online & Free Courses

03

IIT Kharagpur · NPTEL / Free

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur

Architectural Conservation and Historic Preservation

A free online course via NPTEL addressing the concepts of historic preservation and conservation — covering the conservation movement's history, international frameworks, and guidelines for preserving historic buildings. Accessible to anyone with an internet connection.

Free · NPTEL Enrol Free

SWAYAM · Free

SWAYAM National Online Courses

Understanding Heritage

A free SWAYAM platform course covering India's cultural heritage, conservation principles, and the global frameworks that govern heritage practice. Open to students across disciplines, with opportunity for professional development in heritage conservation and management sectors.

Free · Govt. India Enrol Free

UNESCO WHC · Free

UNESCO World Heritage Centre

World Heritage and Sustainable Development Online Resources

UNESCO's World Heritage Centre provides a range of free online learning resources, case studies, and capacity-building materials for heritage professionals and students, including the World Heritage Scholarship and links to affiliated university programmes globally.

Free · UNESCO Explore
