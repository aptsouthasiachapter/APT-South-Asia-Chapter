Who We Are

A Regional Voice for Heritage Preservation

The APT South Asia Chapter (APT-SAC) was established in 2019 as a regional chapter of the Association for Preservation Technology International (APT).

Our mission is to serve the South Asia region through educational and training activities at the local and national level in India, with outreach extending across the broader South Asia region.

We bring together professionals, academics, and advocates committed to the preservation, protection, and stewardship of the built heritage of South Asia.
